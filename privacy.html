Privacy Policy

We keep things transparent and minimal. Here’s exactly what we collect, why, and how we handle it.

Last updated: 1 March 2025 · Effective: 1 March 2025

Grounded Form Stories (“we”, “us”, “our”) operates the website groundedformstories.com. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your personal information when you visit our site or interact with us.

We have written this in plain language because we believe you should understand what happens to your data without needing a legal dictionary. If anything is unclear, please contact us.

1. Who we are

Grounded Form Stories is an independent editorial publication focused on interior design and architecture. Our registered address is:

Grounded Form Stories
Studio 14, 221 Canal Road
London, N1 7UX
United Kingdom

For any privacy-related questions, you can reach us at privacy@groundedformstories.com.

2. What information we collect

We only collect the minimum amount of data necessary to run the website and respond to you. Specifically:

  • Information you give us voluntarily: When you use our contact form, we collect your name, email address, subject, and message content. When you email us directly, we receive your email address and any information you include in your message.
  • Automatically collected technical data: Like most websites, we may collect standard log data such as your IP address, browser type, device type, operating system, referring URLs, and pages visited. This is used in aggregate to understand how the site is used and to keep it secure. We do not use this data to identify you personally.
  • Cookies and similar technologies: We use a small number of essential cookies to make the site work. We do not use advertising, tracking, or third‑party analytics cookies. For more details, see our Cookies Policy.

3. How we use your information

We use your information only for the following purposes:

  • To respond to your enquiries, story pitches, corrections, or other messages.
  • To improve the content and functionality of the website based on aggregated usage patterns.
  • To comply with legal obligations, if required.

We will never sell, rent, or trade your personal information to anyone. We do not send marketing emails unless you explicitly ask to be added to a list (which we don’t currently maintain).

Our legal basis for processing (for visitors in the UK/EU): We process your personal data on the basis of your consent (when you submit a contact form) and our legitimate interests (in operating and improving our website). You can withdraw consent at any time by contacting us.

4. How we store and protect your data

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your personal information from unauthorised access, loss, or misuse. Contact form submissions are stored securely and are only accessed by the small editorial team who need to read and respond to messages.

Emails you send us are stored in our email provider’s secure systems. We retain contact form submissions and emails only for as long as necessary to handle your request, and then for a reasonable period afterwards (typically up to 24 months) in case of follow‑up questions. After that, they are deleted.

Please note that no method of transmission over the internet is completely secure. While we do our best, we cannot guarantee absolute security.

5. Sharing your information

We do not share your personal data with third parties except in the following limited circumstances:

  • Service providers: We may use trusted third‑party services for hosting the website or managing email. These providers only process data on our behalf and are contractually bound to keep it confidential.
  • Legal requirements: If required by law, we may disclose your information to comply with a legal obligation, protect our rights, or investigate potential violations.

We never share your data for advertising or marketing purposes.

6. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Right to access: You can ask us what personal data we hold about you.
  • Right to rectification: You can ask us to correct any inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • Right to erasure: You can ask us to delete your personal data, subject to certain legal exceptions.
  • Right to restrict processing: You can ask us to limit how we use your data.
  • Right to object: You can object to our processing of your data based on legitimate interests.
  • Right to data portability: You can request a copy of your data in a structured, machine‑readable format.

To exercise any of these rights, please email privacy@groundedformstories.com. We will respond within 30 days. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority (in the UK, the Information Commissioner’s Office at ico.org.uk).

7. Children’s privacy

Our website is not directed at children under the age of 16. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe we have inadvertently collected data from a child, please contact us so we can delete it.

8. International transfers

Our website is hosted on servers located in the European Economic Area (EEA). If you are accessing the site from outside the EEA, your data may be transferred to and processed in the EEA. We ensure that any such transfers comply with applicable data protection laws.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or legal requirements. When we do, we will revise the “Last updated” date at the top of this page. We encourage you to review this page periodically. If we make significant changes, we will highlight them on the website.

10. Contact us

If you have any questions, concerns, or requests regarding this Privacy Policy or how we handle your data, please get in touch:

Email: privacy@groundedformstories.com
Post: Grounded Form Stories, Studio 14, 221 Canal Road, London, N1 7UX, United Kingdom

We’re a small team and we read every message — we’ll do our best to resolve any issue quickly and fairly.
