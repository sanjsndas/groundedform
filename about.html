About Grounded Form Stories

An independent editorial publication for people who believe that the spaces we inhabit shape how we live, think, and feel.

Our mission

Grounded Form Stories was founded on a simple conviction: that good design writing should be as considered as the spaces it describes. We publish long-form articles, practical guides, and critical reflections that help readers understand not just what makes a room work, but why.

We are not a trend site. We don't chase viral interiors or seasonal colour palettes. Instead, we focus on enduring principles: material honesty, proportion, light, and the quiet details that make a house feel like home. Our content is grounded in real-world experience — written by designers, architects, and researchers who care about getting the details right.

Whether you're renovating a period property, specifying materials for a new build, or simply trying to make your living room feel more calm, we hope you'll find something useful here.

What we stand for

  • Substance over spectacle. We value depth, nuance, and practical knowledge over quick takes and aesthetic trends.
  • Material honesty. We champion the beauty of real materials — wood, stone, metal, clay — and the way they age with grace.
  • Slow design. Good interiors take time. We write for readers who are willing to think, plan, and invest in spaces that last.
  • Independence. We are editorially independent. We don't accept sponsored content disguised as editorial, and we disclose any commercial relationships clearly.
  • Accessibility. Design knowledge shouldn't be locked behind jargon. We aim to explain complex ideas in clear, readable language.

Editorial policy & information

Editorial independence

Our editorial content is never influenced by advertisers or commercial partners. We do not publish paid-for articles. Any sponsored content is clearly labelled as such. We strive for accuracy and welcome corrections — please contact us if you spot an error.

We occasionally use affiliate links for books or tools we genuinely recommend. This never affects our editorial judgement, and we only link to products we have used or researched thoroughly.

Contact & submissions

We welcome thoughtful questions, story ideas, and pitches from writers and designers. Please email editor@groundedformstories.com. We read every message but cannot always respond individually.

If you are a PR professional or brand, please note that we do not accept unsolicited product samples for review, and we do not guarantee coverage.

Privacy & cookies

We respect your privacy. This site does not collect personal data unless you voluntarily subscribe to our newsletter. We never sell or share email addresses. Analytics are anonymised and used only to improve content. For more details, see our Privacy Policy and Cookies Policy.

Terms of use

All content is provided for general information. We strive for accuracy but welcome corrections. Reproduction of articles requires permission. Links to external sites are provided for context; we are not responsible for their content. See our full Terms of Use.
