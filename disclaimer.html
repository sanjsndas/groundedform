Cookies Policy

We use the smallest number of cookies possible — only what’s needed to make the site work properly. Here’s the full picture.

Last updated: 1 March 2025 · Effective: 1 March 2025

Grounded Form Stories (“we”, “us”, “our”) operates the website groundedformstories.com. This Cookies Policy explains what cookies are, which ones we use, and how you can manage them. It should be read alongside our Privacy Policy.

We’ve kept this simple and straightforward — just like our design philosophy.

1. What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files that are placed on your computer or mobile device when you visit a website. They are widely used to make websites work more efficiently, as well as to provide information to the site owners. Cookies don’t contain any information that personally identifies you, but personal data we store about you may be linked to the information stored in and obtained from cookies.

We also use the term “cookies” broadly to include similar technologies like local storage, which may store small amounts of data on your device.

2. Our approach to cookies

We are a small, independent publication. We don’t run ads, we don’t track you across the internet, and we don’t use any third‑party analytics or marketing cookies. We only use the cookies that are strictly necessary to make the website function properly.

In short: no tracking, no advertising, no profiling. Just the essentials.

3. Cookies we use

The table below lists the specific cookies we use and why:

Name Purpose Duration Type
gfs_session Essential for maintaining your session while you browse the site. It helps remember your preferences (like search filters) during a single visit. Session (deleted when you close your browser) Essential
gfs_consent Stores your cookie consent preference so we don’t ask you again on every page. 1 year Essential
gfs_csrf Security token used to protect forms (like the contact form) against cross‑site request forgery. Session Essential

We do not use any functional, analytics, advertising, or social media cookies. If this changes in the future, we will update this policy and ask for your consent where required.

4. Third‑party cookies

We do not allow third parties to set cookies on our website. However, some pages may include embedded content (for example, a YouTube video or a font loaded from Google Fonts). These external services may set their own cookies or collect data according to their own privacy policies.

We currently use:

  • Google Fonts — to display the typography on our site. Google may collect your IP address and set a cookie. You can view Google’s privacy policy for more information.

We have no control over these third‑party cookies. Please check the relevant third‑party websites for more information.

5. How to manage cookies

You have the right to decide whether to accept or reject cookies. You can set or amend your web browser controls to accept or refuse cookies. If you choose to reject cookies, you may still use our website, though some functionality may be limited.

Here’s how to manage cookies in the most common browsers:

  • Google Chrome
  • Mozilla Firefox
  • Safari
  • Microsoft Edge

To find out more about cookies, including how to see what cookies have been set, visit aboutcookies.org or allaboutcookies.org.

6. Changes to this policy

We may update this Cookies Policy occasionally to reflect changes in technology or legal requirements. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated “Last updated” date. We encourage you to check back periodically.
