Before you write

Can I republish your articles?
We’re happy for you to quote short excerpts with a clear link back to the original. For full reproductions, please email us first. We usually say yes for non-commercial, educational use.
Do you accept sponsored posts?
No. We keep our editorial independent. We do not publish sponsored content, affiliate-driven articles, or paid links. Any partnership would be clearly labelled and rare.
I have a design question — can you help?
We’re a publication, not a design consultancy. However, we often answer general questions in our FAQ or future articles. Send it over — if it’s broadly useful, we might write about it.
How do I submit a correction?
Email us with the article title, the specific error, and a source if possible. We review all corrections and update articles promptly with a note if needed.
